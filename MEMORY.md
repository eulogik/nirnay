## Phase A v1 failure — diagnosis (2026-09-25, all measured)

Chain of causation for 40/3080 = 0.01299:

1. **Scorer starvation**: for c2f rows, delivered logits were `log(c2f full_probs)` (pointer), so the dense [MASK] scorer got zero choice-CE gradient on 10003 banking rows (only 512 synth rows trained it). Trained scorer became a constant function (gold logit 0.094 for every row; rowstd 1e-6).
2. **Stage-1 non-differentiable**: `stage1_retrieve` = cosine top-k — indices block gradient; combined with gold teacher-forcing at train AND eval, stage-1 retrieval never trained and eval was dishonest (gold always in support → measured recall looked fine while honest recall was chance).
3. **Query collapse**: query = mean-pool over full sequence (template-dominated: `[CLS] ins [SEP] [MASK]opts… [SEP] state [SEP]`, only state varies). Trained query pair-cos 1.00000000 (relvar 6.4e-9). Probe: **all 4 pooling variants (full/state-span/markers/CLS) ≈ chance at init** (cent_acc ≈ 1/77) — mean-pooling fundamentally lacks label signal; state-span fix alone doesn't rescue it.
4. **Label-sorted batches, no shuffle**: `batches[step % len(batches)]` over mix ordered synth→train.csv(label-sorted)→test.csv; batches were effectively single-label (history choice_ce artifacts explained).
5. **Test-split leakage**: `build_phase_a_mix` loaded train+test (n_mix 13595 included all 3080 test rows). Irrelevant to the failure (model was degenerate on seen rows too) but invalidates v1 eval.
6. **Identity-at-init violations**: random-init `ByteFusion` (scale 0.1 × LayerNorm(random patches) ≈ 0.1-magnitude noise vs embeddings) and non-residual random `ConceptBottleneck` (replaced h entirely) wiped laya's dense scorer at init: fresh train model top20 recall 0.24 ≈ chance vs raw laya **0.5844** (our encoding's true zero-shot; plan's cited "Laya 0.425" not reproducible with our template — our baselines: dense_acc 0.1429, top20 0.5844).
7. Not defects: ncp negative = entropy bonus by design; `byte.scale/score.3.bias std=nan` = 1-element std artifact; label alignment correct; eval train-vs-eval mode both 3.18 (dropout not the gap).
